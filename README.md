# 🎬 Netflix Data Analysis (EDA Project)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Netflix dataset to uncover patterns in content distribution, audience targeting, and platform growth.

The goal is to transform raw data into meaningful insights that reflect Netflix’s business strategy.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze distribution of Movies vs TV Shows
- Understand country-wise content production
- Identify growth trends over time
- Study ratings and audience targeting
- Explore duration and genre patterns

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib / Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## 📊 Key Insights

### 🎬 Content Strategy
- Movies dominate the platform → indicates focus on short-form engagement

### 🌍 Global Expansion
- USA leads in content production
- Increasing content from India and other countries → global growth strategy

### 📈 Growth Trend
- Rapid increase in content after 2016 → aggressive scaling phase

### ⭐ Audience Targeting
- Majority content rated TV-MA → focus on mature audiences

### ⏱️ Duration Insights
- Most movies are 90–120 minutes → optimized for viewer retention

### 🎭 Genre Trends
- Drama and Comedy dominate
- Rise in international content → localization strategy

---

## 🧠 Business Conclusion
Netflix focuses on:
- Expanding globally
- Increasing content volume rapidly
- Targeting adult audiences
- Optimizing engagement through movie-based content
